The page follows the figure.

Keep the useful bit.
Give it a bigger page.

A wide flowchart shouldn't become tiny type on a portrait page. Plate|Fold measures the figure and finds a page that fits. First landscape, then a larger sheet, then a page cut to its proportions.

The paragraphs around it can stay on Letter or A4. A short diagram stays with the text. A big one gets its own plate. Everything comes out in the same PDF.

Markdown, Word, Excel, CSV and TSV. Headings, tables, local images and Mermaid diagrams stay part of the document.

Legacy .doc, .odt and .rtf need a compatible installed LibreOffice or Pandoc. Plate|Fold re-lays out the content; it doesn't reproduce Word pages.
